The Coalition

A rebel resistance movement fighting to free the fictional nation of Urzikstan from a brutal occupation. Led by Farah Karim, the Coalition partners with Western operators during the 2019 conflict, trading local knowledge of the terrain for support against a far better-armed occupying force.

ALLIED

Task Force 141

A multinational special operations unit reassembled after the fall of its previous incarnation. Operating under Captain John Price, the task force answers to no single government, drawing operators from the SAS and beyond to run deniable missions against threats too large for conventional forces.

IMAGE PENDING
HOSTILE PMC

Shadow Company

A private military contractor built from former special-forces operators, bankrolled through black-market arms deals. Nominally a security firm, Shadow Company blurs the line between contractor and cartel muscle, and its command structure answers to interests far removed from any flag.

IMAGE PENDING
ALLIED

Los Vaqueros

An elite unit of the Mexican Special Forces, led by Colonel Alejandro Vargas alongside with his second-in-command, Major Rodolfo Parra. All soldiers of the unit are handpicked for their loyalty to the country and their skills.

IMAGE PENDING
CARTEL

Las Almas Cartel

A powerful narco-trafficking organization rooted in the fictional city of Las Almas. The cartel's territory and smuggling routes become the battleground for a proxy fight between the task force, a militant faction, and the contractors circling both.

IMAGE PENDING
MILITANT

Al-Qatala

An extremist militant network operating out of the fictional Republic of Al Qatala, formed from the remnants of an earlier insurgency. Its leadership pursues heavy ordnance through cartel connections, pulling the task force's operation across two continents.

IMAGE PENDING
ULTRANATIONALIST

Konni Group

An ultranationalist paramilitary network rebuilt around the freed war criminal Vladimir Makarov. The group stages a prison break to spring its leader, then expands rapidly through open territory, forcing the task force into wide-scale combat operations to contain it.

SECTION III

CAMPAIGN TIMELINE

Key events reconstructed from after-action reports, in chronological order across all three operations.

MW '19 · LOG 001

Urzikstan Falls Under Occupation

A brutal military occupation grips the fictional nation of Urzikstan, with General Barkov's forces answering any resistance with overwhelming force.

MW '19 · LOG 002

A Chemical Attack Is Pinned on the Rebels

An atrocity carried out by the occupying force is framed as an act of terror by Farah's Coalition fighters, hardening international opinion against the people it was meant to help.

MW '19 · LOG 003

Price and Alex Join the Fight

Captain Price and CIA officer Alex embed with the Coalition, helping Farah's fighters turn the tide against Barkov while a separate thread tracks the militant group Al-Qatala.

MWII '22 · LOG 004

A Missile Goes Missing

A shipment of Western-made precision missiles is stolen en route, setting off a search that stretches from Amsterdam back to the Middle East.

MWII '22 · LOG 005

Task Force 141 Reforms

Price reassembles a small, trusted team to hunt the missiles quietly, before the story of who has them becomes a diplomatic incident.

MWII '22 · LOG 006

The Las Almas Connection

The trail leads to a cartel stronghold, forcing the task force into an uneasy alliance with a local special-forces unit fighting the same enemy from the inside.

MWII '22 · LOG 007

Shadow Company Surfaces

A private contractor with its own supply lines into the conflict begins working every angle, and its interests turn out to run deeper than a simple security contract.

MWII '22 · LOG 008

An Uneasy Endgame

The hunt for the missiles collapses into a wider confrontation between the task force, the cartel, the militants, and the contractors — with no side fully in control of the outcome.

MWIII '23 · LOG 009

Makarov Breaks Free

The Konni Group stages a prison break to free Vladimir Makarov, a war criminal whose ultranationalist ambitions have been contained for years.

MWIII '23 · LOG 010

Open Conflict Spreads

Makarov's network expands faster than the task force can track it, pulling operations into sprawling, open combat across contested territory.

MWIII '23 · LOG 011

A Familiar Face Turns

A senior officer with a long history alongside the task force is revealed to have been steering the crisis from behind the scenes, upending who the team can trust.

MWIII '23 · LOG 012

The Task Force Regroups

With Makarov's network scattered and the truth about the betrayal exposed, Price's team is left to pick up the pieces of a war fought on two fronts at once.

SECTION IV

AREAS OF OPERATION

Confirmed theaters of engagement.

Urzikstan

A fictional nation caught between occupation and rebellion — the primary battleground of the 2019 conflict and a flashpoint again in 2023.

Las Almas

A dense, fictional Mexican city and cartel stronghold — the primary urban battleground of the 2022 campaign.

Al Mazrah

A sprawling fictional Middle Eastern region contested by militant forces, contractors, and the task force alike.

Amsterdam

The European city where the search for the stolen missiles first breaks open, in the shadow of its canals and nightlife.

San Ambrosio Cove

A coastal smuggling route used to move contraband beneath the notice of conventional patrols.
