Chronicle

The Chronicle

Both sagas, laid end to end.

I

A Debt to Ares

Deceived into killing his own family by the god he served, Kratos begins a personal war against Olympus that starts with Ares himself.

II

The Titans Rise

Betrayed once again by Zeus, Kratos allies with the imprisoned Titans to storm Olympus directly.

III

The Fall of Olympus

The war against the gods reaches its end, and the age of Olympus effectively closes with it.

IV

A New Life in Midgard

Years later and worlds away, Kratos is raising a son in the Norse wilds, trying to bury who he used to be.

V

Fimbulwinter Begins

A chance encounter sets a years-long winter in motion, putting Kratos and Atreus on a path toward Jötunheim and the gods watching them.

VI

The War With Asgard

Old alliances and new betrayals pull Kratos's family into open conflict with Odin's court as Ragnarök approaches.

VII

Ragnarök Arrives

The prophesied end plays out, reshaping the fate of every realm involved, including Kratos and Atreus's own roles in it.

VIII

Valhalla

In the aftermath, Kratos is drawn into a strange, recursive trial confronting echoes of the enemies and choices from his past, looking for something closer to peace than victory.
