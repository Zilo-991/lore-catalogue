Log Section II

Personnel of Record

Survivors, agents, and one former captain, tracked across successive incidents.

Jill Valentine

S.T.A.R.S. Alpha Team, later BSAA

The Spencer Mansion's most methodical survivor, whose lock-picking and puzzle instincts kept her team alive when firepower alone wasn't going to cut it.

Chris Redfield

S.T.A.R.S. Alpha Team, later BSAA

A former Air Force pilot turned Umbrella's most persistent enemy, following the bio-weapons trail from one continent to the next long after most people would have stopped.

Leon S. Kennedy

Rookie RPD officer, later federal agent

A police officer whose first day on the job happened to coincide with a city-wide outbreak, and who somehow made a career out of surviving the sequel incidents too.

Claire Redfield

Independent investigator, later anti-bioterrorism NGO

Chris's sister, pulled into the same fight from the opposite direction — searching for family first, and finding a conspiracy along the way that she refuses to let go of.

Ada Wong

Freelance operative

A recurring presence at nearly every major incident, working an agenda that never quite lines up with anyone else's — allied, adversarial, or somewhere she alone understands.

Albert Wesker

Former S.T.A.R.S. captain

The mission commander who sold his own team out to watch a bio-weapon in action, and whose ambitions only grow less containable the longer he survives the fallout.

Ethan Winters

Civilian

An ordinary person with no combat background, dropped into successive nightmares for one consistent reason: trying to get his family back home safely.

Rebecca Chambers

S.T.A.R.S. Bravo Team medic

The youngest member of Bravo team, whose field medicine and lab instincts outpace her rank on a mission that was supposed to be routine.

Log Section III

Incident Record

Major containment failures, in chronological order.

FILE 01

The Spencer Mansion Incident

A S.T.A.R.S. team investigating a string of disappearances in the Arklay Mountains discovers an underground bio-weapons lab beneath what looked like an abandoned estate.

FILE 02

The Raccoon City Outbreak

A viral agent escapes containment within city limits and spreads faster than any response can keep pace with, ending in a full evacuation and an airstrike that erases the city from the map.

FILE 03

Exposure of the Antarctic Facility

A hidden research base is uncovered and dismantled as international scrutiny of the responsible corporation finally catches up with its buried infrastructure.

FILE 04

The Corporation's Collapse

Mounting evidence and internal betrayal bring down the pharmaceutical conglomerate behind the outbreaks, though its research and its rivals both outlive it.

FILE 05

The Baker Family Incident

An isolated Louisiana family is transformed by a mind-altering organism, drawing in an outside investigator who wasn't looking for anything more than his missing wife.

FILE 06

The Village Incident

A remote Eastern European village conceals a bio-weapon experiment far older than it looks, unearthed when an ordinary family's night goes catastrophically wrong.
